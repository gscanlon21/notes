**You can have too much of a good thing: Skin fibroblasts shield mast cells from excess Vitamin A**
2014
_[Article](https://www.u-tokyo.ac.jp/focus/en/articles/a_00252.html)_
: "The concentration of vitamin A in the skin is regulated by dermal fibroblasts and when there is excessive vitamin A or fibroblasts cease to metabolise of vitamin A, mast cells become abnormally active and induce skin inflammation."
: "This research suggests that the differing characteristics of mast cells in each tissue type and their abnormal activity are connected with chronic inflammation and allergies that occur throughout the body, and offers a new target for development of preventions and treatments."
