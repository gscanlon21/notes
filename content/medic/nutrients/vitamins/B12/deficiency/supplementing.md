# Dosage and Absorption

> [!TIP]
> Absorption is boosted when B12 mixes with saliva!



**[Dosage and Absorption Tips](https://www.consumerlab.com/answers/whats-the-best-way-to-take-vitamin-b12/best-way-to-take-b12/)**
: > [!WARNING]
  > "Very high-dose B-12 is not recommended for people who do not have trouble absorbing B-12, as there may be side effects."
: "Most importantly, be aware that your body can only efficiently absorb a small amount of B-12 (about 2.4 mcg) at a time."
: - "To absorb more B-12, divide the dose over the course of the day and take separately from a B-12-containing meal."
: - "If you take a higher dose, such as 100 mcg, you will absorb only about 1% of the amount above 2.4 mcg."
: "Research suggests that very high-dose oral supplementation (typically 1,000 mcg daily) can effectively treat B-12 deficiency — even in people who have compromised absorption due to lack intrinsic factor (a cause of "pernicious anemia") or from gastrointestinal disease or bowel resection"
