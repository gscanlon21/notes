# Research

**The physiological and pathophysiological roles of copper in the nervous system**
Gale & Aizenman 2024
_[Review Article](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/ejn.16370)_
: "Recent evidence suggests that neuronal differentiation is associated with increased copper dependence."
: "Increased intracellular copper and the proper trafficking of copper transporters have been found to be essential for neurite extension and synaptogenesis."
: "Dopamine-β-hydroxylase (DBH) is a cuproenzyme critical for the synthesis of the neurotransmitter norepinephrine (NE) in noradrenergic neurons of the locus coeruleus and sympathetic nerve terminals."
: > Requires ascorbic acid and molecular oxygen as substrates (Murthy et al., 1986).
: "Intriguingly, copper may play an important role in the regulation of circadian rhythms."
: "Disorders of copper homeostasis or copper transporters can lead to a wide range of neurological symptoms including parkinsonism, sleep disturbances, seizures, muscle weakness, and ultimately result in neuronal and glial death."


**Remyelination in Multiple Sclerosis: Findings in the Cuprizone Model**
Leo & Kipp 2022
_[Review Article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9783537/)_
: "Cuprizone, chemically known as bis-(cyclohexanone) oxaldehydrozone, is a synthetic chelating compound initially used to detect trace copper [44,45]. This compound became of interest in biomedical research when it was discovered to exert toxic effects in the CNS of laboratory mice [46]."
: "The intoxication of young adult mice with cuprizone, mixed into standard rodent chow in a concentration of 0.2–0.5% (w/w), induces, within some days, oligodendrocyte stress, leading to oligodendrocyte degeneration [47]. This presumably primary oligodendrocyte insult leads to the activation of astrocytes and microglia, the latter phagocytosing the degenerating myelin sheaths. This results in the acute demyelination of distinct white and gray matter brain regions [48]."
: "Since cuprizone chelates copper and copper is an essential trace element for a number of metalloenzymes involved in cellular respiration, it is widely presumed that CNS damage due to the cuprizone intoxication is a result of copper dyshomeostasis and, in consequence, mitochondrial dysfunction. In contrast to this theory, results of a recent study suggest that cuprizone’s toxicity is not due to copper depletion but instead due to a gain of toxicity induced by an unusual cuprizone:copper complex [44]."
: "Whatever the precise underlying mechanism of the cuprizone-induced toxicity is, it triggers a highly reproducible demyelination of distinct white and gray matter regions. Of note, although demyelination is widespread, it occurs in a region-specific manner."
