# Car Accidents

> [!NOTE]
> Call the police if injured, wreckage is in the road, or person is confrontational.

- [ ] Take several pictures of their car, including damage and license plate.
- [ ] Take several pictures of your car, including damage and license plate.
- [ ] Take a few wide angle pictures of the entire scene.
- [ ] Take a picture of their insurance information.
- [ ] Preferably get their name and phone number.

- [ ] Name and phone numbers of any witnesses.
