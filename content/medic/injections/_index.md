# Overview

**Subcutaneous Needles**
SubQ
: Uses a smaller needle to deliver medication to the fatty layer just under the skin.
: Injection Sites
: - Upper Arm
  - Abdomen
  - Thigh

**Intramuscular Needles**
: Uses a larger needle to deliver medication to the muscle.
: Injection Sites
: - Deltoid
  - Quad
