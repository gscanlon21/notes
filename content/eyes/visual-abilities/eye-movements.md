## Vestibulo-Ocular Movements
**Overview**[^1]
: The ability to stabilize your head movements while maintaining focus on an object.

## Vergence
**Overview**[^1]
: The ability to move both eyes together to focus on a specific point.
: "Adjusts for different viewing distance."

**Convergence**
: The inward movement of the eyes to focus on an object at a close distance.

**Divergence**
: The outward movement of the eyes while looking at distant objects.


## Saccades
**Overview**[^1]
: The ability to flick your eyes towards a target.
: "Directs eyes toward visual target."


## Smooth Pursuits
**Overview**[^1]
: The ability to track a moving object along a path.
: "Follows moving visual target."
