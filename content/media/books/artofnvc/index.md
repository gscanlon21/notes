# Nonviolent Communication
**Overview**
: Your feelings come from your own needs, expectations, and perceptions.
: Other people's words and actions might trigger feelings in you, but they cannot cause those feelings.

**Steps**
: 1. [Observe Without Evaluation](#observe-without-evaluation)
  1. [Identify and Express How You Feel](#identify-and-express-how-you-feel)
  1. [Identify and Express Your Underlying Needs](#identify-and-express-your-underlying-needs)
  1. [Make Requests to Meet Your Needs](#make-requests-to-meet-your-needs)

## Observe Without Evaluation

**Overview**
: Separate observations from evaluations. 
: Describe only what you can see or hear. 
: Exclude your interpretations, analyses, or judgments.

**Example**
: > I noticed that you are tapping your foot.
: > ~~Your foot is tapping off the beat of the music.~~


## Identify and Express How You Feel
**Overview**
: Identify and express how you feel due to the observations. 
: Be careful not to confuse your feelings with your thoughts or opinions.

**Example**
: > I feel annoyed.
: > ~~You're being annoying.~~


## Identify and Express Your Underlying Needs
**Overview**
: Take emotional responsibility (rather than blaming others for how you feel).
: Identify your underlying needs or values that lead to your feelings.

**Example**
: > I need peace and quiet to study. 
: > ~~You're interrupting my peace and quiet.~~


## Make Requests to Meet Your Needs
**Overview**
: Convey clear, specific requests that would satisfy your needs. 
: Listen to specific feedback about others' thoughts/feelings in response to what you've just shared.

**Example**
: > Can you please leave the room or stop tapping your foot?
: > ~~For the love of goodness, stop tapping your foot.~~



# Emergency Empathy
**Overview**
: Helps make the other person feel heard, forces them to introspect to answer your question.
: Ask a question about what the other person is feeling to try and understand their perspective.
: Don't project your own feelings onto the other person.

**Example**
: > Are you feeling frustrated because other people aren't listening to you?
: > ~~You seem angry because you can't regulate your emotions.~~
: > ~~You look tired because you haven't slept in days.~~
