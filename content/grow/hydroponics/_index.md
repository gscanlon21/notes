# Terminology

## Abbreviations

**Controlled Environment Agriculture**
CEA
: Producing plants in a greenhouse or other space.                                                                                             

**Daily Light Integral**
DLI
: The sum of photosynthetic (PAR) light received by plants in a day.

**Photosynthetically Active Radiation**
PAR
: The portion of the electromagnetic spectrum between 400-700 nm plants use for photosynthesis.

**Dissolved Oxygen**
DO
: Oxygen concentration in nutrient solution measured in parts per million.                                                                     

**Electrical Conductivity**
EC
: An indirect measurement of the strength of a nutrient solution.                                                                              


## Units

**Parts per Million**
ppm
: A unit that describes dimensionless quantities such as volume fractions. 
: For describing carbon dioxide concentrations it is a molar basis.

**microsiemens per centimeter**
μS/cm
: A unit of measurement for electrical conductivity.

**mole**
: A number of anything equal to 6.02 x 10<sup>23</sup> items. We use it to quantify the number of photons between 400-700 nm of PAR light plants receive.

**moles per square meter per day**
mol/m²/d
: Integrated PAR light                                                                                                                         

**moles per square meter per second**
mol/m²/s
: Instantaneous PAR light                                                                                                                      

**micro-mole per square meter per second**
μmol/m²/s
: Instantaneous PAR light
