Remove flowers as they appear.[^2]

For fresh use, harvest the leaves as they mature—about 6 weeks after planting.[^2]

For maximum flavor, basil should be harvested when flower buds have started to form but before they begin to open.[^1]
Gather the leaves in the morning after the dew has dried, but before the temperature rises.[^1]

[^1]: Iowa State University: Growing and Using Basil
[^2]: https://yardandgarden.extension.iastate.edu/how-to/growing-harvesting-and-drying-herbs
