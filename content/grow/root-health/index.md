#### Abundance
#### _[go back](#assessment)

**0**
: Roots are abundant.

**1**
: Growth is moderate.

**2**
: Growth is poor.

**3**
: Growth is minimal.


#### Branching
#### _[go back](#assessment)

**0**
: Roots are well-branched.

**1**
: Roots are somewhat restricted, with some fine roots visible, but growth is moderate.

**2**
: Roots are restricted, sparse, or lack abundant growth.

**3**
: Growth is minimal.
