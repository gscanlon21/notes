# Fertilizers

## Types

**1-Part Formula**
_Complete_
: Contains all nutrients required for a particular crop or group of crops.
: Adjustments can be made using individual salts, though the general formula should be 'good enough'. 

**2-Part Formula**
: Calcium is separated from phosphate and sulfates to prevent precipitation in the nutrient solution.
: These two parts must be added and dissolved to the nutrient solution separately.

**3-Part Formula**
: Nutrients are divided into three commonly deficient parts for each part of the plants growth cycle.
: Typically divided into micronutrients, a high-nitrogen formula, and a high phosphorus/potassium formula.
