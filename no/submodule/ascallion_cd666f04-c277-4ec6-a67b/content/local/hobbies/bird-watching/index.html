    St. Louis Audubon Society

    Beginner Bird Walks
    These are shorter and focus on the birds in a particular area.
    Bring binoculars and glasses.
    Generally starts at 8am.
    Field Trips
    These are longer and focus on the birds in multiple areas.
    May require driving from stop to stop.
    Bring binoculars and glasses.
    Generally starts at 8am.

    You must register in advance for bird watching trips. Register at least 48 hours ahead.
