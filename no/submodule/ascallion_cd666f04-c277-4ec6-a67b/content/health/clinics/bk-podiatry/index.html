    I was in lab classes that I needed to walk in, so I ended up taking a medical withdrawal from school. Conservative treatment has been unsuccessful, and has included: RICE, orthotics, metatarsal pads, boot, offloading, oral steroids, steroid shot (external), physical therapy (external). Patient has been on crutches since [date] and has difficulty and pain when putting pressure on the foot. As of [date], patients symptoms have trending worse not been alleviated by conservative treatment.
