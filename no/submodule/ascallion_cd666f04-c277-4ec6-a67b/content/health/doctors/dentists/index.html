    Endodontists
    Endodontists are dentists who specialize in tooth pain, disease, and infection.
    Endodontics encompasses the study (practice) of the basic and clinical sciences of normal dental pulp, the etiology, diagnosis, prevention, and treatment of diseases and injuries of the dental pulp along with associated periradicular conditions.
    Periodontists
    A periodontist is a dental specialist who focuses on the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of periodontal (gum) disease and the placement of dental implants.
