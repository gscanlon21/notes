    Small Talk: Discussion Cards

    Family Discussion Cards

    • What makes someone family?
      • Who is in your family?
    • How can you tell when someone in your family is mad?
      • Look in the mirror and make a mad face. What does your face do?
    • How can you tell when someone in your family is happy?
      • Do a random act of kindness for a family member.
    • What is unique about your family?
      • Share a special talent.
    • What makes you proud of your family?
      • Share something interesting about your family’s past.
    • What holiday traditions do you like celebrating with your family?
      • If you could invent a new holiday, what would it be like?
    • Do you have close friends who feel like family? Who are they?
      • Write a letter to a friend or family member who lives far away.
    • How does your family show that they care?
      • Show someone that you care by saying something nice about them.

    Feelings Discussion Cards

    • Share a story about a time you were very happy.
      • What are 3 things that make you happy?
    • What helps you feel better when you are sad?
      • What would you do if your friend was sad?
    • What does your body feel like when you are worried?
      • Share something that is worrying you right now.
    • What does your body feel like when you are angry?
      • How do you act when you feel angry?
    • What is something that scares you?
      • Tell a scary story.
    • How do you know when someone in your family is upset?
      • How would someone else know that you are upset?
    • When you feel sad, what does your body feel like?
      • How do you act when you feel sad?
    • How does your body feel different when you are worn-out or tired?
      • What is your bedtime routine?

    My World Discussion Cards

    • How does the world change during your favorite season?
      • What is your favorite time of year? Why?
    • If you could be an animal, what would you be?
      • What animal are you most afraid of?
    • What is your favorite song?
      • Can you play an instrument?
    • What kind of wildlife have you seen near your home?
      • What animals have you touched? What did they feel like?
    • What is the farthest place you have traveled to?
      • Where in the world would you most like to visit?
    • What are your favorite games to play outside?
      • Have you ever invented a new game? If so, explain the rules.
    • If you planted a garden, what fruits and vegetables would you grow?
      • Draw a picture of your dream garden.
    • If a magical genie granted you one wish, what would you wish for?
      • What do you think your best friend would wish for?
