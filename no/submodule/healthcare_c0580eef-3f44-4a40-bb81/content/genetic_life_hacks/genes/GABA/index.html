    Key Takeaways

    • GABA (gamma-Aminobutyric acid) is an inhibitory neurotransmitter that acts to stop a neuron from firing. This is an essential way that the brain regulates impulses.
    • Low GABA levels are linked to anxiety and PTSD and may affect immune response.
    • Genetic variants can alter your GABA levels and increase susceptibility to mood disorders.
