    Key Takeaways

    • Autoimmune diseases are caused by your immune system targeting and attacking cells in your body. This can result in a number of different problems: joint pain (rheumatoid arthritis), scaly, thick skin (psoriasis), hypothyroidism (Hashimoto’s), and more.
    • CTLA4 keeps the immune response under control and not attacking your own cells.
    • Variants in CTLA4 increase the risk of a number of autoimmune diseases.
