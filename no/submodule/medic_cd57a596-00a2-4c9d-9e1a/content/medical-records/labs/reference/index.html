    Terminology

    Alkaline Phosphatase
    An enzyme found in your body, mostly in your liver and bones.

    An underactive thyroid gland (hypothyroidism)

    Low red blood cells due to a vitamin B12 deficiency (pernicious anemia)

    Zinc or magnesium deficiency, Hypophosphatasia (HPP)

    Wilson’s disease, a rare genetic disorder that causes the body to make too much copper

    Globulins
    Globulins are crucial for liver function, blood clotting, and fighting infections.

    Total globulins may increase in some chronic inflammatory diseases (such as TB and syphilis), multiple myeloma, collagen disease, and rheumatoid arthritis.

    Decreased levels of globulins are seen in hepatic dysfunction, renal disease, and various neoplasms.
