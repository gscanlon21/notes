    Exam 1 Study Guide

    Be familiar with all of these topics and be able to apply them to questions. For the exam you will be given a period table and a formula sheet to use.

    Unit 1.1

    • Understanding rules for significant figures and rounding
    • Converting numbers between scientific notation and standard notation
    • Identifying differentiating characteristics
    • Interpreting and drawing cooling curves
    • Understanding energy differences in the different physical states of matter
    • Interpreting and drawing phase diagrams
    • Interpreting and drawing vapor pressure curves
    • Different separation techniques and what they’re used to separate
    • Understanding the relationship between intermolecular forces and phase changes

    Unit 1.2

    • Assumptions of the particulate model of matter
    • Listing the different SI units and what they measure
    • Assumptions of the kinetic molecular theory of gases
    • Using the kinetic energy formula
    • Understanding the relationship between particle movement speed, temperature, and particle mass

    Unit 1.3

    • Reading the periodic table
    • Identifying charges on the period tables for ionic compounds
    • Identifying ionic and molecular compounds
    • Identifying diatomic elements
    • Naming compounds
    • Writing molecular formulas from molecule diagrams
    • Determining number of moles of individual atoms in molecular compounds
    • Converting between moles, grams, and particle/ atom amounts
    • Calculating molarity
    • Ideal gas law equation and the relationship between the variables involved

    Math

    • Check provided formula sheet
    • Know what all the formulas are used for
    • Know what the variables represent in each formula
