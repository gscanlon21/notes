    Terminology

    Homozygote
    AA or aa
    Heterozygote
    Aa

    n indicates the number of genome copies.

    Cohesins
    Keep sister chromatids together.
    Ploidy
    The number of complete sets of chromosomes in a cell.
    Aneuploidy
    An abnormal number of sets of chromosomes.
    • Due to mitotic nondisjunction (spindle fiber/anaphase error).
    • Trisomy, Monosomy, Nullisomy, Tetrasomy.
