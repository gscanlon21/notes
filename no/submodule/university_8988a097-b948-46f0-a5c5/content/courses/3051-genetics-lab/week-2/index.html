    Notes:

    Meiosis I

    Prophase I Stages
    Leptotene, Zygotene, Pachytene, Diplotene, Diakinesis

    Metaphase I

    • Homologous pairs line up on a metaphase plate.
    • Independent assortment: 50–50%
    • Tetrads — pairs of homologous chromosomes.
    • Euchromatin — mostly unpacked
    • Heterochromatin — tightly packed
    • Nucleosome → chromatin → chromosome (hetero, eu)

    Recombination

    • Recombination (crossing over) in Prophase I
      • Chiasmata — crossing over points
    Cohesin
    Keeps tetrads together until anaphase I
    Shugosin
    Protects CEN — center of tetrads.

    Aneuploidy

    • Aneuploidy due to non-disjunction
      • Meiosis I → all daughter cells aneuploidy
      • Meiosis II → half daughter cells have aneuploidy
    Synapsis
    The pairing or joining of homologous chromosomes.
