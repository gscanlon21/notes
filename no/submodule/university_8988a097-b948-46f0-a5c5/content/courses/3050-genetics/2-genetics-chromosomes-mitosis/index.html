    Terminology

    Microtubule Organizing Center (MTOC)
    Birthplace of microtubule spindles, from the centrosomes.
    Metaphase Plate
    The center line where the chromosomes line up during metaphase.
    Kinetochore
    A protein wrapped around the centromere and binds to the microtubule spindles.
    Microtubules made of tubulin protein attach to centromere at kinetocore.
    Protein complex around the centromere.
    "Beads on a string"
    Beads are the nucleosomes.
    DNA is wrapped around histones.

    Cell Cycle

    Condensed chromosomes only exist in metaphase, otherwise they are loosely condensed in order to be read.

    Telophase
    Nuclear membranes reform around split chromosomes.
    Cytokinesis
    Plants form vesicles where metaphase plate is that forms a new cell wall.
    Animals cleave the cell in two along the metaphase plate using actin.
