    Terminology

    Non-Recombinant
    Parental type chromosomes.
    Synapsis
    Pairing of homologous chromosomes.
    Chiasmata
    Protein structures where recombination occurs.
    Tetrad Bivalent
    One pair of homologous chromosomes.
    Recombination
    Crossisng over of chismata.
    Recombinant
    The recombined chromatid.
    Non-recombinant
    The non-recombined chromatid.
    Cohesion Protein Meiosis and Mitosis
    Keeps sister chromatids together.
    Separase protein Meiosis and Mitosis
    Separates them and degrades cohesion.
    Shugososhin Meiosis
    At centrometere protects cohesion in anaphase I.
    Sister chromatids stay together until anaphase II.
    Psuedo-Autosomal Region PAR
    How XY chromosomes pair up???
    SRY Gene
    Male-deterining gene.
    Can be caught up in recombination.

    Possible Gametes

    • Number of unique offspring possible.

    2^n, where n is the number of heterozygous genes.

    • 3 genes each: AABBCc = 2^1 = 2 possible gametes.

    AaBbCc = 2^3 = 8 possible gametes.
