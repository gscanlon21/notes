    Independent Assortment

    A double homozygote (AAbb) on the same chromo only has 2 possible gametes, but four with recombination.

    Recombination

    Terminology

    CentiMorgan
    Percent recombinant progeny.

    Overview

    Linked Genes
    Genes that appear on the same chromosomes.
    Are physically connected and usually move together (ex. recombination).
    Independent assortment does not apply.
    Offspring ratios reject ratios expected from independent assortment (chi-squared).
    • Would reject 9:3:3:1 ratio of an F2 dihybrid cross.
    • Would reject 1:1:1:1 ratio of a dominant-recessive test cross.
    • F2 generation is more likely to resemble the P (non-recombinant) generation.

    Types of Linkage

    Complete Linkage
    Genes are near touching and crossover is unlikely to separate them.
    Incomplete Linkage
    Genes are far away and crossover will possibly separate them.

    Phenotypic Classes

    Percent Recombinant

    • centiMorgans (cM)

    • distance between genes.

    Parental Classes
    • When looking at F2 offspring, if ratios align with parent phenotypes then the phenotypic classes are parental.
    Recombinant Classes
    • When looking at F2 offspring, if ratios do not align with parent phenotypes or the independent assortment, then they are recombinant classes.

    One test cross can have a mix of parental and recombinant classes.

    Recombination

    : Use a test cross to test recombinant frequencies. Expected ratio of phenotypic classes is 1:1:1:1.

    Recombination
    More likely to occur between genes that are further apart on the same chromosome because there are more points for chiasmata to attach.
    Recombinant frequencies range between 0% and 50%.
    • 50% recombinant frequency mimics independent assortment.

    Divide total recombinant offspring by total number of offspring to find percent recombinant offspring (cM).

    Recombinant Frequencies

    0%, 0cM extreme
    For very tightly linked genes, there is no recombination.
    50%, 50cM extreme
    Some genes are so far apart that recombination occurs every meiosis.
    This mimics independent assortment.

    The ratio of recombinant gametes to offspring is 1:2. 76% recombinant gametes = 38% recombinant offspring.

    Crossing Over

    Single Crossover
    Two crossover gametes and two normal gametes from a double heterozygote.
    • Non-crossover (parental) offspring are common.
    • Crossover (recombinant) offspring are rare.

    How Geneticists Write Alleles

    A and B are linked and a&b are linked.

    • Aa Bb
    • A/a B/b
    • AB / ab
    • A - B
      a - b

    Phases

    Coupling cis
    AB/ab = AaBb
    Repulsion trans
    Ab/aB = AabB
