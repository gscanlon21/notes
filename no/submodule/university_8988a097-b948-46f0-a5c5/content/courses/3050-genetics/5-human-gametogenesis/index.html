    PAR
    Psuedo-Autosomal Region
    Region on the XY chromos that have a lot of homology - at telomeres.
    Sex tetrad may recombine at the PAR
    • SRY gene - male determining gene - may get swept up in the recombination and blur gender.
    • The SRY gene is very close to, but not in the autosomal region.

    Gametogenesis

    Male

    Spermatogenesis
    Spermatogonium -sperm them cells (2n)
    Primary spermatocyte (2n)
    Secondary spermatocyte (meiosis 1, 1n)
    Spermatids (immature sperm) (meiosis 2, 1n)
    • Sperm are mature
    Female Oogenesis
    Oogonium - stem cells in fetus, 2n
    Primary oocyte (2n)
    Meiosis 1 stops when female baby.
    Secondary oocyte completes during ovulation (generates dud first polar body).
    Stops in metaphase II
    Ovum, 1n completes upon fertilization (generated 2 duds, second polar body).
    Polar Body
    Unviable gametes, not enough cytoplasm.
    Only 1/4 gametes are viable.
    Primary Oocytes
    Still 2n, pause for up to 50 years ??
    • Trisomy 21 is viable, chance increases as maternal age reaches 35.
    Fertilization
    Joining of sperm and ovum.
    Meiosis II
    Not complete in egg until fertilization.
    • Egg has mechanisms to prevent polyspermy. - multiple sperm that fertilize the same egg - zona polusala.
    In vitro fertilization - or genotype the polar body
    Human embryo grows in culture for awhile.
    Can take a single cell from the 8-cell stage embryo to do genotyping.
    Pronuclei
    Nuclei of sperm and egg come together and merge.
    • Can use polar bodies from embryos derived by IVF to genotype for genetic disorders before implanting.
    • Polar body 2 is most similar to viable games b/c only sister chromatids differ.
