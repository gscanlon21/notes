    Genic balance ratio
    Ratio of X chromosomes to ploidy (haploid = 1, diploid = 2, triploid = 3).
    Not whether the Y chromosome exists or not.

    When doing phenotype calculations, you need to account into account the ratio of sex/gender in the offspring.
