    Types of Dominance

    Complete Dominance
    Dominant allele completely masks the recessive allele
    Incomplete Dominance
    Phenotypes are blended.
    Applies to heterozygotes.
    Red D and white d make a variation/shade of pink.
    Co-Dominance
    Heterozygotes show traits from both alleles.
    Aa = both spikey and round sugars on blood cells.

    Blood

    • Blood has multiple alleles, the A allele, the B allele, and the I allele.
    • We only have two alleles per person.
    Plieotrophy
    1 gene affects 2 phenotypes

    Lethality is recessive.

    Other

    Penetrance
    100% penetrance - genotype always has the phenotype.
    Green a genotype - will you always see the phenotype?
    Transcription factors can affect genotype expression.
    Expressivity
    Severity of expressed phenotypes.

    Shades of color of pigment expression.
