    Mendel

    Only autosomal chromosomes

    Genotype
    2 alleles
    3 genotypes
    homozygous dominant / heterozygous, homozygous recessive
    Phenotype
    2 alleles
    2 phenotypes for dominant/recessive alleles.
    The expressed trait in an organism.

    Dominance

    Complete Dominance
    Dominant allele completely masks the recessive allele.

    Phenotypes

    Expressivity

    Penetrance
    ?

    Different genotypes can produce the same phenotypes.

    Punnet Squares

    • Shows gamete genotypes of parent genes.
    Heterozygote X Heterozygote
    Genotypic ratio = 1:2:1 - 4 total
    Phenotypic ratio = 3:1 - 4 total

    As a fraction, 3:1 reads 3/4 : 1/4, 4 total.

    Multiply fractions by total number of offspring to find approx counts of each phenotype.

    Can check if ratio matches up w/ pop by dividing pop # dominant by # recessive to find ratio amount pop.

    Test Cross

    Use a dominant hetero or homozygote and cross them with a homozygous recessive mate

    • Tells if the parent is homozygous or heterozygous dominant trait.
    Homozygote Dominant
    Would show a 4:0 ratio.
    Heterozygote
    Would show a 2:2 ratio.

    Reciprocal Crosses

    • Is this gene on autosomal or sex chromosomes?
    • Must switch male/female phenotypes in second cross.
    Cross 1
    Female white X male purple
    Cross 2
    Female purple X male white

    Look at the offspring ratio of each cross

    • Ratios are the same -> autosomal chromosome.
    • Ratios are different -> Sex-linked chromosome.

    Other

    For independent assortment - only count heterozygous genes!
