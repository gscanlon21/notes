    Potential Energy

    U

    • Energy of location
    • Only conservative forces have potential energy!
    • w is work!

    delta U = U_b - U_a = -w_{a->b}

    Gravitational Potential Energy

    $$U = mgy + constant$$

    • The constant is equal to zero and may be dropped

    Spring Potential Energy

    $$U = 1/2kx^2 + constant$$

    • Constant is equal to zero and may be dropped

    Energy has no absolute zero value - depends on reference frame/constant. Can only calculate absolute difference between U_b and U_a

    Mechanical Energy

    • Sum of kinetic and potential energy.

    $$E = K + U$$

    Change in Mechanical Energy

    $$deltaE = deltaK + deltaU$$

    $$deltaE = w_total + -w_conservative forces = w_non-conservative forces$$

    • Change in mechanical energy is zero if there are no non-conservative forces (no friction).

    • Conservative forces conserve mechanical energy.

    Projectile Motion Problems

    • K + U remains constant as projectile moves.
    • As object moves up, kinetic energy gets converted into potential energy.
    • Where kinetic energy equals 0 = the highest point in the trajectory.

    Conservation of Energy

    Only applies for a system experiencing only conservative forces. Particle moves from point A to point B.

    U
    Can either be gravitational or spring potential energy.

    $$\frac{1}{2}mv_A^2 + U_A = \frac{1}{2}mv_B^2 + U_B$$

    $$K_A + U_A = K_B + U_B$$

    Power

    Power
    Unit of power is Watt (W)
    1 Watt = 1 Joule/second.
    $$power = energy / time$$
    $$1 W = 1 J/s$$
    Rate at which energy is consumed or generated.
    Relation to Energy
    $$energy = power * time$$
    $$J = W * s$$
