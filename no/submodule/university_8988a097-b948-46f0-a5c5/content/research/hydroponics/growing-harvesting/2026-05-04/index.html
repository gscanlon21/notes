    Propose

    The purpose of this lab was to adjust the pH of my fertilizer solution to around 5.5

    Procedure

    • Pipette 1ml of potassium carbonate (GH pH Up) at a time, stir, and wait 5 minutes before re-checking pH.
    • pH will be checked with 4.5-8.5 pH test paper.

    Results & Observations

    • Starting pH measured around 4.75.
    • Pipetted 1ml of potassium carbonate (GH pH Up) into 1700ml fertilizer water, pH rose to 5.25.
    • Pipetted 1ml of potassium carbonate (GH pH Up) into 1700ml fertilizer water, pH rose to 5.75.

    Conclusion

    If future fertilizer solutions experience a pH crash, then 1-2 ml of potassium carbonate should be enough to bump it back into range.
