    Purpose

    The purpose of this lab is to make four jars of fertilizer solution.

    Procedure

    Changes Made

    1: Fill Containers
    1. Fill four 64 oz mason jars with 1700 mL distilled water.
    2. Add 1.417 g of fertilizer for a target 100 ppm Nitrogen.
    3. Wait at least 5 minutes then measure and note EC levels.
    4. Do a preliminary check of pH. If above 5, stop and check for the expected pH crash the next day.
    2: Adjust pH
    1. If the pH is under 5.5, pipette 1 mL of potassium carbonate (GH pH Up) at a time, stir. Wait 5 minutes and re-check pH.
    2. Cap the jars and wait a day before planting to re-check pH.

    Materials

    • pH will be checked using 4.5-8.5 pH test paper from Micro Essentials Lab (Hydrion).
    • Use a transfer pipette for pH Up adjustments.
      • Rinse with DI water in-between adjustments and rest in a DI water beaker.
    • Use a plastic straw to stir.
