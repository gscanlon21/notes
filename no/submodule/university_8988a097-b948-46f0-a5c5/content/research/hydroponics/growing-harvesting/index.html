    PPM Fertilizer Calculations

    _Jack's Nutrients 12-4-16 RO

    mg of fertilizer = target ppm (mg L) / % of desire element in compound

    = mg of fertilizer in 1 liter of water to supply target ppm

    50 ppm N .47 L of distilled water
    50 ppm / .12 % N * .47 L
    0.196 g of nutrient in .47 L
    100 ppm N 1.7 L of distilled water
    100 ppm / .12 % N * 1.7 L
    1.417 g of nutrient in 1.7 L
    125 ppm N 1.7 L of distilled water
    125 ppm / .12 % N * 1.7 L
    1.771 g of nutrient in 1.7 L
