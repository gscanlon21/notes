    PPM Fertilizer Calculations

    _Jack's Nutrients 12-4-16 RO

    mg of fertilizer = target ppm (mg L) / % of desire element in compound

    = mg of fertilizer in 1 liter of water to supply target ppm

    Divide by 1000 twice to get grams of fertilizer per ml of water.

    50 ppm N
    470 ml Water
    50 / .12 / 1000 / 1000 * 470
    0.196 g nutrient
    100 ppm N
    1700 ml Water
    100 / .12 / 1000 / 1000 * 1700
    1.417 g of nutrient
    125 ppm N
    1700 ml Water
    125 / .12 / 1000 / 1000 * 1700
    1.771 g of nutrient

    Hydrogen Peroxide

    A spray bottle of Dawn PowerWash gives out exactly 1 ml of hydrogen peroxide per full-spray.

    If you use an undiluted 35% hydrogen peroxide, the recommended dose would be 2-3 Milliliter for each gallon of water.

    With 3% hydrogen peroxide at your disposal, you should begin by adding 3 milliliters per liter or 2-3 teaspoons per gallon of reservoir water.
