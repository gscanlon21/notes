    Fertilizer Solution Disposal

    Ask Emily about a fine-mesh strainer.

    Fine-Mesh Strainer

    1. Autoclave the fertilizer solution and jars.
    2. Dump them outside on plants using a strainer to collect and loose grow medium.
    3. Throw the loose grow medium in the trash.

    Lettuce Storage

    Make sure lettuce is completely dry before storing it.

    Label each container with:
    • My Name
    • Date Made
    • What's Inside
    • "Not for Consumption"
    Storage Ideas
    Store in a brown paper bag thats folded down tight, but not clipped tight.
    Store in a part-zipped plastic bag with a paper towel.
    Store in an airtight plastic bag with a paper towel.
    Wrap tightly in aluminum foil.
    Store dry in an airtight jar.
