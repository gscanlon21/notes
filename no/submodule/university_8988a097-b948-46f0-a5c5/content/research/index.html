    Thesis Defenses

    Thesis Defense: Joyce Kang This talk does not include questions
    How do we leverage many large scale-scale single-cell datasets to better understand immune-mediated diseases.
    • We set out to understand HLA genes, which are critical for immune-mediated diseases.
    • We developed two methods to do this in single-cells:
    • scHLApers – a personalized pipeline to quantify HLA expression
    • Symphony – a general-purpose tool for integrating multiple datasets by reference mapping
    • By combining four cohorts, we created a cross-tissue atlas and mapped eQTLs for classical HLA genes in diverse immune cell types.
    • Single-cell eQTL models revealed dynamic HLA regulation and pinpointed their cellular contexts.
    • Regulation of HLA expression levels may contribute to biologically important and disease-relevant inter-individual variation in immune responses.
