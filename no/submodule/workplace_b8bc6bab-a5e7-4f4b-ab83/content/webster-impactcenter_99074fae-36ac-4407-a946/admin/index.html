    Approvals

    Notification Settings

    Teams > Settings > Notification and Activity > Scroll Down to 'Apps' > Approvals > Expand Dropdown

    Automating Approvals
    In PowerAutomate, create a trigger with 'SharePoint: Item is Created'.
    Make the action 'SharePoint: Create an Approval Request for Item or File'.
