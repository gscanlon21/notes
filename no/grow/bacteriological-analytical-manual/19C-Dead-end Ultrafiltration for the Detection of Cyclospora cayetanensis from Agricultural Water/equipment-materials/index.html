    Equipment and Supplies

    • Geopump™ Peristaltic Pump Series II Package (includes easy-load II pump head and a portable battery), Geotech, Cat No. 91352123
    • Pump Assembly (or equivalent assembly):
      • DIN adapter (for End Port), Molded Products, Cat. No. MPC855 NS.375
      • Blood port (End) cap, Molded Products, Cat. No. MPC-40
      • Dialysate Port (Side) cap, Molded Products, Cat. No. MPC-60D
      • L/S 36 tubing (9.7mm ID), Cole Parmer, Cat. No. EW-96410-36 or EW-06434-36
      • SNP-8 hose clamps (for DIN adapter), Cole Parmer, Cat. No. EW-06832-08
      • SNP-12 hose clamps (for side port), Cole Parmer, Cat. No. EW-06832-12 (optional)

    • Turbidity Meter (optional) with a resolution of 0.01 NTU, a range up to at least 200 NTU, and compliant with EPA 180.1 method or ISO 7027 standard.

    • Appropriate Personal protective equipment (PPE) such as hand sanitizer, gloves, protective eyewear, and lab coats as needed.

    Recovery and Detection of Cyclospora cayetanensis from Ultrafilters

    _Part II

    Laboratory Facilities

    • A laboratory bench for the ultrafilter backflushing and concentration procedures.
    • A laboratory bench or hood for the DNA extraction procedure.
    • Separate workstations for each step of the qPCR protocol to allow an efficient workflow and eliminate the potential for positive results due to laboratory contamination.
